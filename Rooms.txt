Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
vacuuming.
Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your
bedroom, and east to the kitchen.
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
occupies the far corner.
The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of
clothes. You can return south to the living room.
#
Office
This is where you work VERY hard to make lots of cash money. There is a
very out-dated laptop on your corner desk. The window overlooks the backyard.
A sign reading "Best Dad Ever!" lays on the ground. 
You can return east to go back to the loving room.
#
Kitchen
You have made it to the kitchen! This is where your mom would make... questionable
meals for your family. Dishes are piled up to the ceiling, most would question
how that is even possible. The floor is covered in peanut butter, but no jelly
to be found :O
Living room door is West, The door reeking of gasoline that leads to the garage
is to your south.
#
Attic
You can barely crawl around in this attic! Nails poke through the roof with very
threatening aura. There are dust bunnies in every corner and this fluffy stuff
that seems like cotton candy... Don't eat the cotton candy.
You can head down to the bedroom at any time!
#
Garage
A car leaking many kinds of fluids sits in the center. Balls and tools are scattered
around the floor. Everything seems to be dirty, dusty, and stinky. It would be in
your best interest to clean this place out sometime!
You can head north to the kitchen
